The meetings at Rosebank keep giving me a push. They remind me that I probably don't share and spread the gospel as much as I should. One night recently, Brody's message made me stop and think about hell, what it meant to me before I was saved, and what it means to me now.

<!--more-->

Before I was saved, I couldn’t have cared less about hell. I didn’t care that I could end up there, and I never seriously asked whether it was even real. Worse, I would wish it on people. I said it carelessly, as if it were a punchline, with no thought that I was talking about real people and a real place.

Now I believe it, and it saddens me. There are people there now, and more will end up there. The Lord Jesus spoke of it plainly.

> **Matthew 10:28 (KJV)**
>
> *“And fear not them which kill the body, but are not able to kill the soul: but rather fear him which is able to destroy both soul and body in hell.”*

Those are the words of the same Saviour who showed such compassion to sinners. He didn’t speak of hell to frighten people for the sake of it. He spoke of it because it is true, and because He doesn’t want anyone to go there.

God doesn’t want it either.

> **2 Peter 3:9 (KJV)**
>
> *“...not willing that any should perish, but that all should come to repentance.”*

That is what makes it all the more sad. Nobody has to go. The way out has already been paid for at the cross, and it is as simple as it is free.

> **Romans 6:23 (KJV)**
>
> *“For the wages of sin is death; but the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.”*

What humbles me most is that I once didn’t care about any of this. Now that I do, I can’t keep it to myself. The Lord Jesus Christ is the way people don’t have to end up in hell, and I want to be more faithful in telling them about Him.
